\section{A model and its rate of change}
The inline model is \(f(x)=x^2+2x+1\). Its derivative is
\begin{equation}
  f'(x)=2x+2.
  \label{eq:derivative}
\end{equation}
Equation~\ref{eq:derivative} gives \(f'(1)=4\).

\begin{align}
  \int_0^1 x^2\,\mathrm{d}x
    &= \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^1 \\
    &= \frac{1}{3}.
\end{align}

For \(\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^2\), a linear transformation can use
\[
  A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{bmatrix},
  \qquad
  g(x)=
  \begin{cases}
    x^2, & x\geq 0,\\
    -x, & x<0.
  \end{cases}
\]
An example speed is \(\qty{2.0}{\metre\per\second}\).
